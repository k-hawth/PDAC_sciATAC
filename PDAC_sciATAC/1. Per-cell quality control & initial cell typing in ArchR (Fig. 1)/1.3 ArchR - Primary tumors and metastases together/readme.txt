# Note: The scripts listed in this section (Section 1.3) cover the multi-step quality filtering of primary tumor and metastatic cells in ArchR. Cells that pass filters (see 1.3.1 to 1.3.3) are used for MACS v3 peak calling (Section 2.1) and CNV estimation (Section 4). The epithelial/malignant subset of these passing cells are used for epigenomic analysis of epithelial/malignant cells (Sections 5-7).
